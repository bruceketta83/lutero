# Prompt per gli sfondi (Gemini)

Sfondi del **prologo** e del **capitolo 1**. Leggi prima [GRAFICA.md](../GRAFICA.md).

## Come usare i prompt

1. Apri una chat nuova per ogni sfondo.
2. Allega sempre `originali/sfondi/tipografia.jpg` come riferimento di stile.
3. Incolla il **blocco di stile comune**, poi il prompt della scena.
4. Scarica in PNG alla massima risoluzione.
5. Dai al file il nome indicato (es. `strada.png`).

## Blocco di stile comune

```
Black and white seinen manga illustration in a dark fantasy ink style: dense
cross-hatching, heavy solid black shadows, dramatic chiaroscuro light, detailed
realistic pen linework, soft grey ink washes for fog and distance.
Historical setting: Saxony, Germany, early 16th century. Realistic architecture,
mud roads, half-timbered houses, Gothic churches, heavy skies.
Background for a 2D point-and-click adventure game, seen from the side, eye level.
NO colour at all, only black, white and grey, unless a colour is listed below.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no frame, no panel borders,
no speech bubbles. Empty scene: no people unless listed.
Dark, gritty mood: deep black shadows in the corners and on the ceiling, rough
aggressive pen strokes, dust and smoke in the light beams. Plain papers and
signs, with no lines of text.
Keep the walkable ground clear and flat in the lower third of the image.
Keep important objects away from the left and right 10% of the image.
Landscape 16:9, 2576 x 1438 pixels.
```

## Capitolo 1 — Wittenberg e Jüterbog, 1517

**4. `tipografia.png` — La tipografia di Mastro Grunenberg (interno)**
```
Interior of a small early 16th century printing workshop. In the centre-right,
a large wooden printing press with a big screw and a long lever. On the left,
slanted wooden type cases with many small compartments. Stacks of paper,
printed sheets hanging to dry on strings across the ceiling, ink balls on a
table, an oil lamp, a small window with grey daylight. Wooden floor, worn and
stained with ink. Warm but dim light from the lamp.
```

**5. `mercato.png` — La piazza del mercato di Wittenberg**
```
The market square of a small German town in 1517, overcast spring day.
Half-timbered houses with steep roofs, the town hall at the back, a stone well
in the centre-left, a few empty wooden market stalls with cloth roofs, mud and
straw on the cobblestones. Some dark silhouettes of townspeople far in the

**6. `convento-cortile.png` — Il cortile del Convento Nero (agostiniani)**

The inner courtyard of a plain, austere Augustinian monastery in Wittenberg.

**7. `cella.png` — La cella di Lutero**

A small, bare monk's study room in a monastery. A heavy wooden writing desk

**8. `chiesa-castello.png` — La Chiesa del Castello (esterno, il portone)**

The side of a large Gothic castle church in Wittenberg, with a tall pointed

Inside a Gothic church: a side hall full of reliquaries displayed on long

A country road through flat fields and birch woods in spring. On the right, a

**11. `jueterbog.png` — La piazza di Jüterbog con la croce dell'indulgenza**

The square of a small medieval German town in front of a church. A wooden

**12. `titolo.png` — Schermata del titolo (verticale)**
```
Portrait 9:16, 1440 x 2560 pixels. A young girl of 13 in a simple dress and

up a freshly printed sheet of paper towards a window. Through the window, a
violent thunderstorm and a lightning bolt over the roofs of a medieval town.
Only colour: a small pale blue ribbon in her hair (#7fa7c9). Leave the top 20%

## Controllo del risultato

1. Bianco e nero puro. Unici colori: lo stemma rosso a Jüterbog, il nastro azzurro nel titolo.
2. Nessuna scritta, nessuna firma, nessuna cornice.
3. Nessun personaggio in primo piano (solo sagome lontane dove indicato).
4. Il pavimento è libero e piano nel terzo in basso.
5. Gli oggetti importanti non stanno nel 10% a destra e a sinistra.
6. Formato 16:9 (titolo 9:16).
